Web Development

This course is designed to provide Multimedia Engineering students with an understanding of and practical experience in front-end web development. Over the course of the program, students will progress from the fundamentals of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to the advanced use of libraries and frameworks such as React.js.

The course combines theory with practice including both individual and team projects to ensure students can apply what they have learned in real-world contexts.
